◈ The Process

From brief to delivery in four steps.

  1. 01

    Brief

    The client shares the brand, tone and monument or destination. No 20-page deck needed: a reference and an objective is enough.

  2. 02

    Production

    Historical research, sequence generation, best shot selection, rhythm editing and audio. 5–7 calendar days.

  3. 03

    Review

    One round of revisions included in all services. Two rounds in UGC packs and the monthly retainer.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final file in publication resolution, with usage rights according to the contracted plan. For channel reels: direct publication.

◈ The Stack

The stack matters. The eye, more.

The tools in use include state-of-the-art video generation models: Google Veo, Kling AI, and audio synthesis systems like ElevenLabs. None of these tools produce good results on their own — they require judgment, iteration and manual editing at every stage.

Each published reel involves between 30 and 80 discarded generations before arriving at the final shot. The prompt is just the beginning. What differentiates the result is the eye deciding what works and what doesn't.

As an experienced drone operator, Lucas also brings real aerial footage into the pipeline — a layer of authenticity and cinematic control that purely AI-generated content cannot replicate.

The exact prompts and production workflow are not shared. That is part of the value of the service.
